Do topic-model clusters survive an embedding classifier?

Let LDA sort 2,594 posts into clusters from word counts alone. Then ask a classifier that has only seen the sentence embeddings to rebuild those clusters. Where the two views disagree, is that a meaningful set of documents — or just noise?

Where the two views part ways

The disagreements are readable

Held out from the whole pipeline, the true newsgroup acts as a referee. On the disagreement set the embedding pick matches the true group 47.9% of the time versus 42.6% for the LDA cluster it overrode. The reassignments are net-corrective — and they read as documents whose words point one way while their meaning points another:

LDA · comp.graphics→ embed · sci.med  referee true · sci.med ✓

“huge good mri sets big cost millions dollars wants reading scan … gordon banks … pitt edu”

A post about MRI scanners. The words image / edu / file pull bag-of-words toward graphics (θ = 0.92); the embedding reads medical imaging.

LDA · sci.space→ embed · sci.med  referee true · sci.med ✓

“cut medical newsletter page volume number april colorado source … reported cases increased comparison united states”

An epidemiology bulletin filed under space on data / research; the embedding recognizes it as medicine.

LDA · sci.med (generic)→ embed · sci.space  referee true · sci.space ✓

“thought emergency conditions sts good size orbiter just need don know need pat”

Short, chatty Shuttle post that LDA dropped in the generic bucket; the embedding pulls it back to space.

187 of the 390 disagreements are cases like these — the embedding matches truth where the LDA cluster missed. A smaller tail is genuinely garbled posts neither view places.

Does it hold at full scale?

The clusters stay strongly learnable — 73% cross-validated recovery against a 26% base rate. Two independent views of a 20-way partition still agree on nearly three-quarters of documents.

The interesting flip

At 20 groups the embeddings track LDA’s clusters as well as the real labels.

At 5 groups the true newsgroups were the easier target (0.899 vs. 0.850). At 20 they swap: embeddings recover LDA’s content clusters (0.729) about as well as the official groups (0.725).

Several of the 20 official groups overlap heavily — the two comp.sys.* hardware groups, the three talk.politics.*, the Christian / atheism / religion-misc trio. LDA groups by content, not by those administrative seams, and the embedding geometry backs content grouping at least as much as the taxonomy (which the clusters best-map to at only 0.39). The LDA topics are clean regardless — space, sci.crypt (key / encryption / clipper), hockey, guns, C source code, Mideast politics.

Still a strong diagonal

The disagreements: meaningful, but diluted

Disagreements still skew to LDA’s low-confidence docs, and many are real semantic corrections — but two things change at 20-way, and both are worth naming plainly.

LDA · talk.politics.mideast→ embed · rec.sport.hockey  referee true · hockey ✓

“world championships germany group standings munich … canada czech republic russia finland italy sweden usa”

A hockey standings post. The flood of country names pulls bag-of-words toward geopolitics; the embedding reads it as sport. The clean kind of correction.

LDA · cluster 3 (junk)→ embed · generic  referee neither — garbage

“max max max bxn bxn giz max max qax nrhj biz air air bhj bhj …”

Caveat 1 — the junk confound. 20NG’s notorious uuencode/ASCII spew forms its own high-confidence LDA cluster (θ ≈ 0.99). The classifier can’t place gibberish, so these dominate the most-confident disagreements without being meaningful. Drop the junk + generic clusters and 737 content-vs-content disagreements remain; 233 are clean embedding corrections.

Caveat 2 — the referee goes quiet. With the true label as referee, the embedding pick and the LDA cluster now match the truth about equally on the disagreement set (24.1% vs. 25.5%). The clean “embedding corrects the topic model” win from 5 groups flattens to a tie — not because the disagreements are noise, but because at 20-way the ground truth itself is ambiguous: when politics.misc, politics.guns, and religion.misc bleed together, “the right group” is barely defined, and both views sit near the 0.39 best-map ceiling.

So — a meaningful set?

Yes, at both scales — with an honest asterisk at 20. The different-cluster documents are the genuinely ambiguous, boundary-straddling posts, concentrated on LDA’s low-confidence calls and full of legible cases where sentence meaning corrects a bag-of-words slip. At 5 groups the embedding is also measurably more right than the topic model it overrides.

At 20 groups that clean win dilutes: degenerate junk posts crowd the top of the list, and because the official newsgroups themselves overlap, the ground truth stops being a sharp referee. The technique still does its main job — surfacing a corpus’s hard, confusable documents as a short readable list — you just read past the junk cluster and accept that some disagreements are two defensible answers to a genuinely ambiguous document, not a right and a wrong one.
